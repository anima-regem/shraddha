Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 July 2026

Shraddha is a UPSC preparation app. This policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

Information stored on your device

Shraddha does not require an account and the developer does not operate a server for your study data. The app stores your study progress, flashcard schedules, mock results, daily-goal preference, appearance preference, optional local Supporter-moment count, and configured content-repository URL locally on your device.

Optional content sync

If you enter a GitHub repository URL in Settings and choose to sync, Shraddha requests the study-content files from that repository over HTTPS. GitHub handles those requests under its own privacy policy. The repository URL and sync metadata remain stored locally on your device.

Optional advertising

Shraddha shows Google AdMob advertising only when you voluntarily open Settings, choose Support Shraddha, and open the Watch ads page. That page may offer a banner, a clearly labelled native sponsored card, and a rewarded video that you explicitly choose to watch. No advertisements are requested or shown in study screens, flashcards, practice, mocks, progress, or other Settings surfaces.

When you use the Watch ads page, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as the app name, an advertising identifier, IP address, and device or interaction information to deliver, measure, personalise where permitted, and protect advertising from fraud. Google describes this processing in its partner-services information page and Privacy Policy.

Where required, Shraddha presents Google's consent choices before requesting an advertisement. The Watch ads page also provides a Privacy choices entry point when Google requires one. We do not receive or store the information Google processes for ad delivery.

External links

The app can open GitHub and Buy Me a Coffee in your browser when you choose those links. Those websites operate under their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

You can remove local study-progress data with Settings, Reset all progress. Clearing Shraddha's app storage or uninstalling the app removes its locally stored preferences and database. Google and any linked third-party service retain information according to their own policies.

Security

Shraddha stores app data in the operating system's app storage and uses HTTPS for optional content-sync requests. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Shraddha's features or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the latest version.
